## Design

Two complementary curves form a single S, representing two people managing household money together. The palette follows the app: forest green `#12463A`, warm ivory `#F5F3EF`, and soft lime `#D9F2A6`. This is a flattened PNG logo concept; it is not a layered Icon Composer asset. The existing installed app icon is unchanged.

The direction follows Apple's current guidance on simple silhouettes, minimal shapes, a full-bleed background, and leaving dynamic material effects to the system:

- [App icons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons)
- [Icon Composer](https://developer.apple.com/icon-composer/)
